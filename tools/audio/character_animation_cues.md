# Character animation SFX

Original synthesized Foley and magic, generated by `synthesize_character_animation_sfx.py`.
No voice clips or reference-video audio are used. WAV assets live under
`ManosabaLin/audio/character_animation/`; the listening reel and sample validation report
are written to `.local/character_animation_sfx/`.

All cue indices below are **zero-based displayed frames**, inspected against the current
scene's actual atlas references. Every referenced frame has duration 1.0. Do not use the
13-frame `.local/arisa_attack6` preview: the scene uses the 18-frame fireball animation.

- Ema: palm circle; shield impact flash; floor circle followed by light forming at the hand.
- Hiro: red sword trail; raised sword guard; red light climbs the upright sword then brightens.
- Sherry: fist ring; bracing under impact; cyan fist charge followed by the pressure burst.
- Arisa: fire appears at the hand; crossed-arm flame guard; palms ignite then the red aura flares.
- Anan: paper friction followed by a light flight sound ~0.07 s later (visual ray at frame 7);
  recoiling with the book; writing followed by the open book's text light.

The real character sprite owns one controller and four persistent audio voices. Card-driven
and RitsuLib-driven animation paths share it; cross-fade sprites do not. Short files preload
with the character asset list and are retained before combat animation begins. Cues use the
displayed frame, not asynchronous timers. On an interrupted action, prior audio fades in
55 ms. The second cast cue fades the preparation sound; a normal return to idle keeps the
short release tail. Scene removal stops all voices. Existing card-specific FMOD sounds remain.

Validation: offline PCM header, peak and endpoint checks; C# compilation. Game playback and
subjective listening still require review; the reel is provided for that purpose.
